II.  About the Wanderer

[joa]

I wouldn’t call myself a poet. I’m just a wanderer with no map, no destination, and a pen. These days that pen is a phone with a keyboard, because it’s the 21st century and my handwriting was never going to win anyone over.

I write in English and Khasi/Pnar, mostly after dark, and mostly with rum. ill be honest, rum is the real creative director here. Without it I stare at a blank screen and think about another peg. With it, I get cold balconies, cigarettes burning down to ash, and deep questions that show up when nobody’s looking. Sometimes they even rhyme.

sur lyng-a is simply my notebook on the move, as well as my summer intership project.

no rum was harmed in the making of this website (it was).
