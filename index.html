II.  About the Wanderer

[joa]

I don't really consider myself a poet. I’m just a wanderer who happens to carry a pen or in this digital era, a phone with a keyboard, walking without a map and with no intention of stopping for now.

I write between English and Khasi/Pnar, most of what’s here comes from late nights, cold balconies, cigarettes burning down to ash, or drinking rum and the questions that surface when nobody is looking.

sur lyng-a is simply my notebook on the move, as well as my summer intership project.

Typography

Display type set in Cormorant Garamond by Christian Thalmann. Body text in EB Garamond, a digital revival of Claude Garamond's roman types of the 1530s. Metadata and catalog numbers in Courier Prime by Alan Dague-Greene.

Publication

sur lyng-a is a digital chapbook published in continuous edition. Poems are added as written. All work © [joa], all years. No part of this publication may be reproduced without the author's written permission. Except for quotation in critical articles, reviews, and lonesome marginal notes.

Find Me

Correspondence by electronic mail is welcomed. I respond slowly and thoughtfully, which I believe is the correct pace for letters.

This site has no tracking, no cookies (except your theme and bookmarks, saved locally), and no interest in your data. Only your attention, and only if you'd like to give it.
